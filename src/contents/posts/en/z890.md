The Intel Z890 PCH provides fantastic connectivity, even though LGA1851 lasted only a single generation. It's a real tragedy that such an excellent product has gone unnoticed.

## Background Information

The table below gives the originally designed connectivity of Arrow Lake (Refresh) CPUs and Z890 PCH.

*Table 1. Theoretical Connectivity of Z890 Motherboards*

Notes: 

1. Display Interfaces are only available for non-F SKUs, available outputs are:
	1. `eDP1.4b`, with 3840x2400 @120Hz max.
	2. `DP 2.1 UHBR 20`, with 7680x4320 @60Hz max.
	3. `HDMI 2.1 FRL`, with 7680x4320 @60Hz max (4096x2304 @60Hz for HDMI 2.1 TMDS).
2. `Thunderbolt 4` also features `DP 2.1 UHBR 20` and `USB @20Gbps`.
